Welcome to Family Matters S.P.A

You do not have to face life's challenges alone.

Life's challenges can affect our emotional well-being, relationships, and sense of purpose.

Whether you are navigating grief and loss, relationship difficulties, emotional distress, or significant life transitions, you do not have to face these challenges alone.

At Family Matters S.P.A Counseling & Consulting Services, we believe that every individual and family deserves the opportunity to experience emotional wellness, healthy relationships, and a renewed sense of hope.

Led by Dr. Sonia M. Smith, PhD, LCSW-R, our Brooklyn-based practice provides compassionate, culturally responsive, strengths-based mental health counseling and therapeutic support for individuals, couples, and families. Our approach recognizes the importance of the whole person, including emotional, relational, cultural, and, when desired by the client, spiritual dimensions of well-being.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Where is Family Matters S.P.A Counseling & Consulting Services located?

Family Matters S.P.A is a Brooklyn-based practice that serves individuals, couples and families in all five New York City boroughs: Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens, the Bronx and Staten Island.

Who leads the practice?

The practice is led by Dr. Sonia M. Smith, PhD, LCSW-R, Lead Therapist, who provides compassionate, culturally responsive, strengths-based mental health counseling.

What kinds of counseling do you offer?

Individual, couples, family and group counseling, including grief and loss support, trauma-informed therapy, cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT), and support for anxiety and depression. We also provide LGBTQ+ affirming care.

Is spirituality part of counseling?

Our approach recognizes the whole person, including emotional, relational, cultural and, when desired by the client, spiritual dimensions of well-being. Clients of every faith and background are welcome.

What is the difference between therapy and consulting?

Counseling and therapy are clinical services. Life transitions consulting, community retreats, creative arts and professional development are non-clinical offerings and are not psychotherapy.

What is Soaring Past Adversities (S.P.A.)?

S.P.A. is the guiding mission of Family Matters S.P.A: to destigmatize mental health, strengthen families, promote resilience, and empower individuals to move beyond life's adversities toward healing, personal growth, and renewed purpose.

How do I schedule a consultation?

Use the Schedule a Consultation button or visit the Contact & Appointments page to call or request an appointment.
